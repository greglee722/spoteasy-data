How many listings are these Back Bay rent figures based on?

Spot Easy's July 2026 figures for Back Bay are based on 592 active listings — studio 203, 1-bedroom 269, 2-bedroom 90, 3-bedroom 30. Spot Easy publishes a median only where a bedroom size has at least 20 active listings in the month.

How does Spot Easy calculate Back Bay rent figures?

Median asking rent is the middle advertised price — half of listings ask more, half ask less. Spot Easy takes every listing active in Back Bay during July 2026, removes drafts, admin-removed listings and asking rents below $500 or above $20,000, de-duplicates by address and unit, and reports the median for each bedroom size with at least 20 listings. These are asking rents, not signed-lease prices.

How we calculate this

Each month Spot Easy snapshots every active listing across Greater Boston, excludes drafts, admin-removed listings, and asking rents below $500 or above $20,000, dedupes by address + unit, and normalizes bedroom counts into Studio / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4+. We require at least 20 active listings in a cell before publishing its median; thinner cells are suppressed. Figures are median asking rents, not signed-lease prices.
