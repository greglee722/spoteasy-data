Questions about this data

Where does Spot Easy's rent data come from?

From Spot Easy's own live listings database — active rental listings posted or verified by licensed real estate agents across Greater Boston, including listings sourced through their brokerages. These are asking rents on real listings, not survey responses, estimates or modeled figures.

Is this average or median rent?

Both. Average (mean) asking rent leads every page, because it is the figure most people mean when they ask what rent costs. Median is published alongside it in the JSON feed and the CSV, and the month-by-month history table is median. Average runs higher than median in Greater Boston, because expensive listings pull the top of the market.

What does "as of September 2026" mean?

It means the figures were published in September 2026 and cover listings that were on the market during August 2026. We publish on the 1st of each month for the month that just ended, and every page names both dates.

Can I republish these numbers?

Yes, including commercially. The data is licensed CC BY 4.0 — credit Spot Easy and link back to the page you took it from. Journalists who need a cut we do not publish can email Joel Mundele, CEO, at joel@spoteasy.com.

Why is my neighborhood missing?

A neighborhood and bedroom size is published only when at least 20 active listings support it that month. Below that, a median moves on one or two listings and is not worth publishing, so we suppress the cell rather than print a number we would not rely on ourselves.

How Spot Easy Collects This Data

How we collect this data +

Source: All data is pulled from Spot Easy's live listings database — active rental listings posted or verified by licensed real estate agents, including listings sourced through their brokerages.

Geography: Greater Boston — Boston, Brookline, Cambridge, Somerville, Medford, Newton, Everett, Quincy, Watertown, and Malden.

Listings counted: Every listing that was on the market at any point during the reported month, measured from its on-market and off-market timestamps — not a single-day snapshot. Drafts, admin-removed listings, and asking rents below $500 or above $20,000 are excluded. A listing that left the market without a recorded off-market date is excluded rather than estimated, so the count runs conservative.

Deduplication: One listing per unique address + unit + bedroom count, keeping the most recently created record.

Bedroom buckets: Studio / 1 BR / 2 BR / 3 BR / 4+ BR.

Minimum sample size: Cells with fewer than 20 listings are suppressed and shown as —.

Metrics: Average (mean) and median asking rent, 25th and 75th percentile, month-over-month and year-over-year change. Average leads on the published pages; median is carried in the CSV and JSON feed.

  • Month-over-month: same neighborhood × bedroom cell from the previous month's snapshot.
  • Year-over-year: same cell from 12 months prior.
