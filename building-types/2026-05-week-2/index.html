    Boston has a mix of housing stock you won’t find in most cities — large apartment buildings, multi-family homes, brownstones, condos, single-families. The price differences by building type are bigger than most renters realize. Active listings only, deduped by address + unit + bedroom count.

    How we calculate this

    Each listing is classified by the building_type field set by the listing agent. Active listings only (is_on_market = true), rents $500–$20,000, deduped by address + unit + bedroom count keeping the most recent listing. Multi-Family covers duplexes, small apartment buildings, and other multi-unit residential properties. Cells with fewer than 15 active listings are suppressed.
