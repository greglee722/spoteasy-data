    Cost per person: solo studio vs splitting a 2BR

    Per-person cost = neighborhood 2BR median ÷ 2. The roommate premium is what you "pay" to live alone in that neighborhood. Active listings only, deduped, $500–$20,000 rent filter.

    NeighborhoodSolo studio2BR ÷ 2Monthly savings% saved
    How we calculate this

    For each neighborhood we take the median asking rent for a Studio and a 2BR. The per-person cost of a roommate-shared 2BR is the 2BR median divided by 2. "Monthly savings" is the studio median minus the per-person 2BR. Neighborhoods need at least 10 active listings in each bedroom bucket to appear.
