    How we calculate this

    Tour request volume from Spot Easy renters who completed a profile, expressed as week-over-week change. We exclude scraped/manual leads from the Working Pro / Student split because those leads do not have a job_status value. Neighborhoods with fewer than 20 tour requests this week are suppressed. We compare against the prior week's window.
